Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy covers this Cosmere Archives website and the Archives of Arcanum workspace app maintained by cosmerearchives-creator. Archives of Arcanum's current public source is the Mind-Map repository. Future apps will identify their applicable privacy policy before release.

1. This website

This is a static information website hosted by GitHub Pages. Its pages contain no analytics, advertising scripts, account registration, or forms collecting workspace data. GitHub receives the network information needed to serve the site, such as your IP address and request details, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following a link to another service subjects that visit to that service's practices.

2. Workspace information on your device

Archives of Arcanum stores Boards, Stickies, text, titles, tags, connections, nested-board references, images and imported content, layout preferences, and related workspace metadata locally. The app uses IndexedDB and a localStorage fallback where applicable. Recovery copies and sync metadata can also be stored locally. Exported backups contain workspace content and are saved wherever you choose. Protect those files accordingly.

The reviewed app does not require a Cosmere Archives account or send workspace data to a Cosmere Archives-operated server. Optional cloud sync, AI requests, updates, and external content involve the services described below.

3. Google account connection and Drive access

The application was renamed from Silverlight to Archives of Arcanum on October 30, 2026. Existing Google consent screens or Drive Manage apps entries may still say Silverlight until their branding is updated separately. Select the existing connected app; the rename does not require a new account or cloud workspace. Legacy local storage paths remain unchanged.

Google Drive connection is optional. Android uses Google Play services authorization. Windows opens Google authorization in your system browser using PKCE and a local loopback callback. Google handles your sign-in; Archives of Arcanum does not receive your Google password.

The requested scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This permits access to Archives of Arcanum's hidden app-data folder, rather than general access to your ordinary Drive documents. The app also retrieves your Drive user permission ID to identify the connected account and prevent mixing sync histories between accounts. It does not request Gmail or Contacts access in the reviewed sync implementation.

What is transferred and why

When you upload a cloud copy or enable live sync, Archives of Arcanum sends workspace snapshots and, for larger transfers, content parts to Google Drive. These can include the text, images, imported content, and relationships in your workspace. Snapshot metadata includes device/platform information, workspace and record IDs, timestamps, revision hashes, record counts, and synchronization history such as deletion markers. The app lists and downloads its snapshots to restore or synchronize your workspace. Google receives the network and authorization information necessary to process these requests.

Live sync operates while the app is open and online and may resume on later launches when enabled. Manual cloud copies are separate retained snapshots. Sync uses Google Drive directly from the app's native integration; it does not route your workspace through a Cosmere Archives cloud server.

Credentials and protection

Access tokens are held by the native app rather than included in the web workspace or board exports. On Windows, a refresh credential is saved locally using Windows user-bound DPAPI protection so the app can renew access, and the selected Desktop OAuth configuration is copied to the app's local settings folder. Android authorization is handled through Google Play services. Local sync metadata stores the connected account's permission ID.

Drive requests use HTTPS. The app uploads JSON snapshots and content parts without an additional end-to-end encryption layer. Do not treat cloud copies or ordinary local backups as encrypted vaults.

4. Optional AI and external content

If you choose Generate with AI, Archives of Arcanum sends your request and a limited text context from the current Board directly to OpenAI using your supplied API key. The reviewed implementation includes up to 75 cards, with title and body text truncated for context. The key is held in page memory for that session rather than saved in Boards or exports. This action is separate from Drive synchronization; Drive sync itself does not send snapshots to OpenAI. Content downloaded from Drive and then used in a Board can be included in a later AI request if you choose that feature. OpenAI processes the request under its privacy policy and applicable API terms.

External links, remote images, imported media URLs, and link previews may make requests to the relevant websites or media hosts. Those hosts can receive your IP address and request information. Update checks and installation downloads contact GitHub and may use platform runtime-download services. These requests are distinct from Google Drive sync.

5. Sharing and use of Google data

The reviewed sync code uses Google data to provide cloud copies, restore, account matching, and synchronization. It contains no advertising, sale of Google data, or developer-operated analytics pipeline. Workspace snapshots are stored in your Google account's app-data area, not published to GitHub. Optional AI and external-content requests are described above. If you submit an issue or support message, the information you choose to provide is available to its recipients; GitHub issues are public.

6. Retention, stopping sync, and deletion

  • Pause sync: choose Pause live sync in the cloud menu on each device. Wait for any active transfer to finish. This stops automatic synchronization on that device but does not revoke Google authorization or delete stored copies.
  • Revoke access: remove Archives of Arcanum's access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access; it does not itself erase local workspaces or necessarily remove cloud data.
  • Delete cloud copies: use Google Drive's Settings → Manage apps → Archives of Arcanum (or the legacy Silverlight name) → Options → Delete hidden app data, where offered. Pause sync on every device and revoke access to prevent later uploads. See Google Drive storage help.
  • Delete local data: use your operating system's app-data controls or remove the app's local data after exporting anything you need. On Windows, workspace and settings data may remain after uninstalling; remove Archives of Arcanum's app data and WebView2 data only when you intend to erase it. The saved Windows refresh credential is %LOCALAPPDATA%/Silverlight/google-drive-session.bin. Deleting it while Archives of Arcanum is closed removes that saved credential; also revoke access at Google.
  • Backups and recovery: separately delete exported backups from their chosen locations. Local recovery and sync history may preserve earlier content even after a Board is deleted. Cloud snapshots are append-only in the reviewed implementation: there is no automatic expiration or in-app cloud-delete operation. Older copies can therefore retain deleted content until you remove the hidden app data.

Local data remains until removed or cleared. Cloud data remains subject to your deletion choices and Google's storage practices. No fixed automatic retention period is implemented for snapshots or local recovery copies.

7. Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through this website's GitHub Issues or Archives of Arcanum Issues. Use a general description; do not post sensitive content, tokens, or API keys. GitHub sign-in is required to submit an issue.

Product naming updated October 30, 2026; this does not represent a new data-handling audit. This policy describes the implementation reviewed on October 24, 2026. Changes to app features or data handling should be reflected here with an updated effective date. Review release notes for version-specific behavior.
