About the archive

One home. Many possibilities.

Cosmere Archives is the shared app hub maintained by cosmerearchives-creator. Silverlight is the first featured app; future apps will have their own pages, downloads, and privacy details.

Silverlight began as a passion project by a creator dissatisfied with their previous mind-map app. Its main-branch code is largely AI-generated under human direction, with real time and costs behind development.

The goal is a full offline-first core with optional sync, AI, and accounts. Independent creators can monetize their work without a success-based fee. Donations and shout-outs are welcome and entirely voluntary.

Community licensing and support explains small-team qualification, grandfathering, and separate commercial licensing.

An independent software project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Brandon Sanderson, Dragonsteel, Google, or Microsoft. Silverlight here is the Cosmere Archives workspace app.
